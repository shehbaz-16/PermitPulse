Example 1

One basement permit, a dozen jobs

A homeowner in Woodbridge pulls a permit to finish the basement as a legal second suite with a side entrance. That single permit is paid work for up to 14 different services over two to four months. Each contractor sees it with a reason written for their service and a timing hint, so bin rentals reach the owner in week one and painters mail a few weeks later, right when they're being hired.

Most owners already have a general contractor or designer for the main job. The follow-on services are often still open, and that's where PermitPulse helps.

The same permit also shows up for landscapers, deck builders, electricians and pool-service companies, each with their own reason.

Straight answers

What PermitPulse is, and what it isn't

It is

  • Fresh permit activity in your area, matched to your services
  • A fast way to put your offer in front of active projects and their neighbours
  • A simple pipeline to track which permits turn into quotes and jobs

It isn't

  • A homeowner phone or email list. We never collect personal contact details.
  • A guaranteed job. Some homeowners will already have hired every service they need.
  • Secret data. Permits are public; we save you the searching and do the outreach.

FAQ

Questions contractors ask

There's no phone number. How do I reach the homeowner?

With a postcard or letter addressed to "Current Resident" at the permit address, plus optional neighbours and a local social campaign we run for you. Homeowners who respond contact you through the QR code or voucher, so every enquiry comes with their consent.

Haven't they already hired someone?

Often for the main job. The follow-on services, like the fence after a pool or landscaping after a new build, are frequently still open, and PermitPulse shows you which ones.

Can't I just check the city website myself?

You can. PermitPulse saves you the daily search, filters to your services, explains why each permit fits, and turns it into a mailed offer in two clicks.

Will my competitors get the same permits?

On Starter Scout, other subscribers offering your service can see the same permits. Exclusive Neighborhood Pro caps each service at two PermitPulse contractors per city and locks your chosen neighbourhoods.

Where does the data come from?

Public building-permit records from the Cities of Markham, Toronto and Vaughan, supplied through a licensed data provider (BuildData). PermitPulse is independent and is not endorsed by any of these cities.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial, and you can cancel before it ends without paying anything. After that, monthly plans can be cancelled any month; yearly plans run for the year you paid for.
